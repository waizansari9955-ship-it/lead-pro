Chapter 1

🔍 How to Use the AI Lead Finder Tool

The Lead Finder Tool allows you to extract real-time targeted business leads filtered by Industry, Country, and City.

  • Select Target Industry: Choose from Real Estate, IT Companies, Digital Marketing, Healthcare, etc.
  • Choose Location: Select Country (India, US, UAE, UK, Canada) and dependent City (e.g. Delhi, Mumbai, Dubai, London).
  • Click Generate Leads: The extraction engine pulls business details, full office addresses, and client requirement intent signals.
Chapter 2

🚀 Generating AI Sales Pitches for Clients

Turn leads into paying clients using our automated AI proposal script generator.

  • Click "Pitch Client": Open the interactive AI proposal modal for any generated business lead.
  • Customized Proposals: The system automatically drafts a personalized cold outreach script tailored to the client's specific requirement.
  • 1-Click Copy & Email: Use Copy Pitch Message or Send Email Now to instantly contact prospects.
Chapter 3

💾 Real-time Firestore Database Storage

All captured leads from both the Home Page Hero Form, Contact Form, and Lead Tool are synchronized directly into your Firebase Firestore Database.

Firestore Collections Structure:

• Collection: "leads" -> Captures organic form submissions from Home & Contact pages. • Collection: "extracted_leads" -> Stores extracted business leads along with Full Address, Client Requirements, and Data Source metadata.
Chapter 4

🌐 Data Sources & Verification

Where does the lead data come from?

  • Google Places & Maps API: Real-time verified physical business addresses and contact details.
  • Public Web Registries: Justdial, YellowPages & Local Business Directory signals.
  • Client Intent Signals: Live hiring & RFQ posts indicating active client requirements.
